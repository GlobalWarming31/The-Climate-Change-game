# The Climate Change Game 🌍🚀

A 2D turn-based artillery game, Command your faction (USA or RUSSIA), calculate the perfect trajectory, factor in the wind, and launch nukes across procedurally generated cityscapes to defeat your opponent! 

Play offline against an AI with adjustable difficulty, or battle a friend in online 1v1 multiplayer.

## ✨ Features
* **Procedurally Generated Cities:** Every match features a uniquely generated skyline of varying skyscraper heights and randomized lit/unlit windows.
* **Destructible Terrain:** Missed shots blast craters into the buildings, permanently altering the battlefield.
* **Offline AI Mode:** Practice your aim against an AI opponent. Adjust the AI difficulty from 1 (easy/random) to 10 (highly accurate).
* **Online Multiplayer:** Connect to a server and battle other players in real-time 1v1 matchmaking.
* **Dynamic Physics:** Your shots are affected by your chosen angle, velocity, and dynamic wind speeds ranging from 1 to 15 mph.
* **Zero External Assets:** All graphics, including the detailed pixel-art soldiers and cityscapes, are drawn dynamically using Python math and shapes—no images required!
* **Fullscreen Support:** Seamlessly toggle between windowed and fullscreen modes at any time.

## 🎮 How to Play
Controls
Menu Navigation:

1 - Start Offline Game

2 - Enter Online Matchmaking

Up / Down Arrows - Adjust AI Difficulty

Game Inputs:

0-9 Number Keys - Type your desired values.

Enter - Lock in your Angle, lock in your Velocity, and Fire.

Backspace - Delete a typo.

Display:

F - Toggle between Fullscreen and Windowed mode.

Mechanics
The Goal: Land a nuke close enough to the enemy soldier to catch them in the blast radius.

Angle: Choose an angle between 0 and 90 degrees.

Velocity: Choose a firing power between 1 and 150.

Wind: Always check the top of the screen! The wind ranges from 1 to 15 mph and pushes left (<--) or right (-->). You must adjust your angle and power to compensate for the wind pushing your nuke off course.
